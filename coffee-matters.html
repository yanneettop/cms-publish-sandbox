Coffee Matters London Cafe Website Design Case Study

Pixel Rebels created the brand direction and website for Coffee Matters London, an independent cafe near Brick Lane. The search-ready site brings the menu, Greek-inspired offer, practical visit information, reviews and private-event enquiries into one warm digital experience.

Updated 15 August 2026 · 5 min read

Project Overview

A local cafe website designed for discovery and action

The project gave Coffee Matters a digital home it could own and grow. Pixel Rebels combined brand direction, responsive web design, locally relevant copy, menu and event content, review presentation and clear directions so first-time visitors could quickly understand the cafe and plan a visit or enquiry.

The Challenge

Turning a local reputation into searchable content

Coffee Matters is a specialty café on Brick Lane, London, founded by three Greek friends. They had a loyal local following but no website — their online presence was limited to an Instagram page and a Google Maps listing. They needed a site that captured the warmth and character of the café, showcased their menu and events, and gave them credibility online to attract new visitors and bookings.

Strategy & Delivery

Website strategy for an independent Brick Lane cafe

Brand direction & visual identity

Refined their brand around the Mediterranean-meets-Brick-Lane personality that makes them distinctive.

Full website design & development

A single-page site with smooth scroll navigation, hero imagery, menu section, gallery, about story, and contact/location information.

Copywriting

All website copy written to reflect their voice: warm, inviting, and proudly Greek.

Google Reviews integration

A clearly attributed reviews section surfaces the current Google rating and links visitors to the original profile, keeping third-party proof verifiable.

SEO foundation

Optimised for local searches like "coffee shop Brick Lane" and "specialty café East London".

Instagram integration

Connecting their active social presence to the website for ongoing content.

Event venue promotion

A dedicated section to drive private event enquiries, opening a new revenue stream.

Outcomes

A clear digital home for local discovery and enquiries

Local search
Clear visit information

Location, opening hours, menu and directions are easy for customers and search engines to understand.

Owned site
Brand-led presence

The cafe can tell its own story beyond the limits of a social feed or map listing.

Venue hire
Commercial enquiry path

Dedicated event content gives private-hire prospects a direct route to ask about the space.

Coffee Matters moved from social and map listings alone to a professionally branded website that explains the offer, supports local discovery and makes both visits and venue enquiries easier. The page treats the cafe's reviews as independent proof, not as a performance result produced by the website.
