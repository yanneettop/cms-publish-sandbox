Ictinus Contractors Website Design Case Study

Pixel Rebels designed and developed a trust-led website for Ictinus Contractors, a London refurbishment and decorating business. The site brings services, project proof, third-party reviews and a direct quote journey into one search-ready digital home.

Updated 15 August 2026 · 5 min read

Project Overview

A contractor website built around trust and direct enquiries

The project turned Ictinus Contractors' offline reputation into an owned digital sales asset. Pixel Rebels combined clear service architecture, location-aware copy, verified review links, project galleries and repeated quote calls to action so prospective clients could understand the offer and enquire without relying on a lead marketplace.

Ictinus Contractors

Strategy, Website, Copy & SEO
The Challenge

Earning trust beyond third-party lead platforms

Ictinus Contractors had more than 12 years of trade experience, verified customer feedback and a growing reputation across London, but no website of its own. Prospective clients had to piece the business together from separate trade profiles. The new site needed to establish credibility independently, explain a broad service offer, showcase completed work and provide a direct route to request a quote.

Strategy & Delivery

Website strategy for a London refurbishment contractor

Brand positioning

Positioned Ictinus as a premium, reliable contractor, not just another painter, with messaging built around trust, quality standards, and verified results.

Multi-page website

Home, About, Services with 7 service categories, Portfolio with 6 projects and gallery, Contact with quote form, and Testimonials.

Third-party review integration

Checkatrade, MyBuilder and Google proof is surfaced with clear attribution and links to the original profiles, helping visitors verify the reputation for themselves.

Copywriting

All website copy crafted to speak to homeowners, landlords, letting agents, and commercial clients, each with their own relevant messaging.

Portfolio with photo galleries

A project-by-project showcase with location, service type, and expandable image galleries.

SEO & local search

Optimised for searches like "decorator London", "refurbishment East London", and relevant service-specific keywords.

Conversion strategy

Multiple CTA placements, strong trust signals, and a direct quote flow built to turn visitors into enquiries.

Outcomes

An owned, trust-led enquiry journey

Owned site
Direct enquiry path

Prospects can review the business and request a quote without starting on a lead marketplace.

Clear scope
Service-led structure

Dedicated content explains refurbishment, decorating and specialist services in a crawlable format.

3 sources
Verifiable social proof

Checkatrade, MyBuilder and Google feedback support trust without presenting ratings as website results.

Ictinus Contractors now has a professional website it controls, with clear services, project proof, attributed reviews and a direct quote request. The build does not claim credit for the company's existing ratings; it uses that independent evidence more effectively and gives prospective clients a clearer path from research to enquiry.
